# CVPilot – AI-Powered Resume & Career Assistant

CVPilot is an AI-assisted career preparation platform designed to help students, fresh graduates, and job seekers improve their resumes and explore relevant career opportunities.

It helps users understand how well their resumes align with a job description, identify skill gaps, improve resume content, and discover potentially relevant job opportunities.

## ✨ Features

### 📄 Resume Analyzer
- Upload and analyze your resume.
- Compare resume content against a job description.
- Identify matched and missing skills.
- Calculate keyword overlap percentage.
- Detect weak action verbs and suggest improvements.
- Get suggestions to improve resume structure and bullet points.

### 💼 Job Recommendations
- Explore job opportunities based on resume-related skills.
- Discover relevant vacancies and career opportunities.
- Find opportunities across different job categories.
- Make job searching more targeted and convenient.

### 🤖 AI Career Assistant
- Get career-related guidance.
- Ask questions about resumes, skills, and job preparation.
- Receive suggestions to improve your career readiness.

### 📊 Resume Insights
- Understand your resume's strengths and weaknesses.
- Identify areas that need improvement.
- Use analysis results to make more informed resume updates.

## 🎯 Why CVPilot?

Many students and fresh graduates struggle to create resumes that communicate their skills effectively and match job requirements.

CVPilot aims to simplify this process by bringing resume analysis, improvement suggestions, and job discovery into one platform.

**The goal is not to guarantee ATS selection, but to help users make their resumes clearer and more relevant to specific opportunities.**

```bash

```

### 2. Navigate to the project folder

```bash

```

```bash

```

Check the available scripts in `package.json` and run the appropriate development command.

